# Topic 2.1 Algorithms: teacher guide

The pack follows the 16 teaching lessons and Lesson 17 assessment in the supplied Algorithms export. Each teaching lesson has six stations and a final challenge, using the existing spherical player.

## Classroom routine

Allow roughly 5 minutes for the Do Now, 5–10 minutes for starter and key terms, 25–30 minutes for the experience and worksheet, then 10 minutes for plenary and reflection. Adapt this to your lesson length. Starter and terminology answers immediately follow their questions. Slide 7 is the combined board workflow. Pupils write station facts in their own words before answering the interactive tasks. Use the provided practical tasks alongside the experience where specified.

## Conventions

- Zero-based list indices. Search comparisons mean comparisons of a stored item with the target, not every Boolean test.
- Binary-search midpoint is rounded down. Lists are ascending unless stated otherwise.
- Bubble-sort passes go from left to right. Sorted-tail optimisation is identified when used.
- OCR-style for loops include the final value. Python range excludes its stop value.
- Room code panels are compact excerpts or worked examples, not always complete runnable programs. Full student algorithms should close each structure and include required initialisation.
- Trace tables may leave unchanged values blank. The teacher should agree whether each row represents a statement or a complete loop iteration before starting.

## Assessment

Use the Lesson 17 worksheet as the 40-mark test. The final page is reflection after marking. Keep slide 3 displayed for the suggested 45 minutes. Reveal the feedback slides only after the test. The mark scheme is a separate teacher file. The experience scores and this topic-test mark are not OCR GCSE grades.
