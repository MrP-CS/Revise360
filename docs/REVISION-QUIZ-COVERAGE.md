# What the revision bank covers

Generated by `tools/revcoverage.py` from `revision/`, so it cannot drift from
the bank it describes. Every number here is a count of what is on disk.

A question is tied to one station of one lesson, and that is where "revisit
this" sends a learner. This file reads that mapping backwards: for each
lesson, how many questions point at it, and which of its stations and
outcomes nothing asks about at all.

## Lessons nothing points at

Every teaching lesson has at least one question against it.

These are revision and challenge lessons. They have stations, but the
stations revise other lessons rather than teaching anything of their own,
so a question sends a learner to the lesson that taught it instead:

`ms-l11`, `ms-l16`, `ms-bonus`, `nw-l07`, `nw-l14`, `ns-bonus`, `el-bonus`, `al-bonus`, `pf-l07`, `rp-l06`, `bl-bonus`

nothing for a question to revisit:

`sa-l05-test`, `ms-l09-test`, `ms-l17-test`, `nw-l08-test`, `nw-l14-test`, `ns-l11-test`, `ss-l05-test`, `el-l08-test`, `al-l12-test`, `pf-l08-test`, `rp-l07-test`, `bl-l04-test`
