Privacy, in plain English

This is the short version. The full data protection policy has the detail your data protection lead will want. Revise 360 is used by children, so it collects as little as possible. No email addresses, no real names, no passwords, no adverts, no tracking, and nothing sold or shared with anyone.

What is stored

That's the lot. The PIN is never sent or stored: only the code made from it. There is no way to recover a forgotten PIN, and a student who forgets one simply starts again.

Nothing here identifies a child by itself. A username only means something to a school that already holds the list. That's deliberate: it keeps the data minimal and the risk low.

What is never collected

  • Email addresses, real names, dates of birth or contact details
  • Passwords: the PIN never leaves the device in a readable form
  • Location, microphone, camera or any headset sensor data
  • Advertising or analytics trackers, and no third-party cookies

Where it is kept, and for how long

Progress is saved in the browser's own storage on the device, and if the school has connected the backend, on a Cloudflare database in the UK and EU. Anything untouched for a year is deleted automatically. A school can ask for a student's data to be deleted at any time, and a teacher can clear it from the dashboard.

Roles under UK GDPR

The school is the data controller: it decides who uses Revise 360 and what usernames are used. Revise 360 acts as a processor for the progress data described above, and only processes it to provide the service. A written processor agreement is provided to every subscribing school, and the data protection policy covers retention, security, sub-processors, transfers, breach handling and DPIA support.

Cookies

None used for tracking. The site stores your sign-in and progress in the browser's local storage so work isn't lost. Clearing site data removes it.

Children

Revise 360 is designed for classroom use with 11 to 16 year olds, under a teacher's supervision. It has no chat, no user-generated content, no public leaderboards and no way for students to contact each other.

Requests and questions

Deletion requests, processor agreements, DPIA support, or anything a data protection lead needs: hello@revise360.co.uk. Requests are answered within one calendar month, and usually much sooner.
