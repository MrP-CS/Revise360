Privacy

The public Revise 360 trial stores demo logins and learning progress in your browser. Shared school accounts and Microsoft sign-in are not yet enabled.

Local demo information

The demo can store student usernames, generated PINs, class labels, progress, answers and scores in browser storage. Teacher demo settings and any colleague details entered into the demo can also be stored there. Use fictional details when trying class-management features.

This local learning data is not currently synced to a Revise 360 database. It stays until removed through the available controls or by clearing the site's browser data. Signing out removes the current sign-in; it does not erase all saved progress or class records. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to access demo data.

Website requests and downloads

The website and its downloadable PowerPoints and worksheets are served through GitHub Pages. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and a 3D library from cdnjs. Requests to these providers can include technical information such as an IP address and browser details; local progress storage does not mean no information leaves the device.

Revise 360 does not include advertising or an analytics tracking script. Its own sign-in and progress features use browser storage. Downloading a resource does not send your completed worksheet or edited slides back to Revise 360.

Contact and access enquiries

If you email Revise 360, we receive the address, name and information you include. We use these to respond to your enquiry and arrange support or access. Please do not send pupil records or passwords. Support emails are retained for up to 12 months, unless an ongoing matter requires them for longer.

The access request form can prepare an email when hosted registration is unavailable. You choose whether to send it from your email application. Your email provider and the recipient's email provider process that correspondence.

Future school accounts

Teacher Microsoft sign-in and hosted class tracking are planned. The privacy information and school processing arrangements will be updated to describe the actual account data, providers, security measures and retention before those features are enabled. The processing agreement is currently a draft for that future service.

Your information and contact

You can clear local demo data using your browser's site-data settings. For information held through correspondence, or to request access, correction or deletion, contact hello@revise360.co.uk. You may also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.
