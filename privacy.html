Privacy

Revise 360 is in development, and at the moment it collects nothing at all.

What happens to your data today

  • There are no accounts, so no names, usernames, email addresses or passwords are collected.
  • Your progress through an experience is stored by your own browser, on your own device, and never sent anywhere.
  • There are no adverts, no analytics and no third-party trackers.
  • Clearing your browser's site data removes everything Revise 360 has stored.

What is coming. Sign-in is being rebuilt around Microsoft school accounts, so students and teachers can use the logins their school already has. When that arrives, the only things stored will be the account identifier the school already holds and each student's quiz progress. This page will be updated, and a full data protection policy and processor agreement will be published, before any of it goes live.

Cookies

None used for tracking. The site stores your progress, and your sign-in, in your browser's local storage.
