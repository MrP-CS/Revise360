FROM STARTER TO PLENARY

A clear learning sequence.

  1. 01

    Recall

    Do Now: research and discuss.
    Start with the key question on slide 1.
    Then the starter and key terms.

  2. 02

    Explore

    Display the task slide.
    Open the experience.

  3. 03

    Explain

    Write key facts in your own words.
    Apply them in the challenges.

  4. 04

    Check

    Complete the plenary.
    Mark, improve and revisit.

Display slide 1 as students arrive: the Do Now question invites immediate research or discussion and focuses the room on the learning ahead. Plan for everyone to reach the plenary towards the end of the lesson.
