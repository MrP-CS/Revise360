Using Revise 360 in the classroom

Bring together a lesson PowerPoint, a matching worksheet and an interactive 360° experience. Teach on a classroom screen, let students explore on a PC or tablet, and use VR where it suits your class.

Your lesson resources

  • Lesson PowerPoint: starter and answers, key terminology and answers, clear experience instructions, and plenary knowledge checks with model answers.
  • Worksheet: a place to record thinking, key facts, challenge answers and exam-style responses in the same lesson sequence.
  • 360° experience: explore the stations, answer questions and apply ideas through interactive challenges.

Download Lesson PowerPoint and Worksheet from the experience selection screen. The slides are available to teachers and students, so they also support revision after the lesson. They contain answers: reveal those slides after students have attempted the questions.

Lesson PowerPoints are being added across the course. Downloads are currently available for Topics 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3; each experience card shows the resources available for that lesson. Standalone games and paper assessments may use a different format.

What a lesson looks like

  1. Starter: display the question, give students time to answer, then move to the answer slide for marking.
  2. Key terms: complete the terminology quiz, then check the definitions on the following answer slide.
  3. Set up the experience: display the combined worksheet and experience instructions. Tell students when to open the experience and leave this slide on the board while they work.
  4. Explore and explain: students work through the stations and write the key facts in their own words. They record their reasoning before answering the interactive questions, then complete the final challenge.
  5. Plenary: bring everyone back together towards the end of the lesson. Students complete the worksheet's exam-style knowledge checks from memory, then use the slides' answers to mark and improve their work.

Plan for everyone to complete the experience and reach the plenary within the lesson. Adjust time at each stage to your class. Students ready to move on can strengthen an explanation or revisit a misconception while the class completes the experience.

Making the resources work together

  • Download and preview the slides and worksheet before teaching. Open the PowerPoint in an application that supports .pptx files and check the layout on your classroom display.
  • Ask for explanations in students' own words, rather than copied sentences from the room.
  • Use the score screen's Secure, Revise and Focus here indicators to identify topics to revisit. Review mode lets students correct mistakes without changing their first-attempt score.
  • Use the blue information markers to deepen understanding where they appear.
  • Because students can download slides with answers, use separate questions when you need an unseen assessment.

VR is optional

The experiences work on a PC or tablet. Immersive VR requires a compatible headset and WebXR browser. Open the experience and choose Enter VR when available; check your school devices before the lesson.

Follow the headset manufacturer's guidance and your school's supervision arrangements. Students should remove the headset for writing and whole-class marking. See the VR guide for controls.

Sign-in and progress: current trial

The public site currently uses a local demo. The teacher dashboard accepts the key demo and lets you try creating usernames and PINs on that browser. Use fictional details for testing.

Logins, class lists and progress stay in that browser. They do not follow students to another device or collect results from a classroom of separate devices. Students should keep their worksheet as a record of their work.

Teacher Microsoft sign-in and hosted progress tracking are planned, but are not available yet. See the privacy information for the current setup.

Answers and teacher support

The downloadable lesson slides include starter answers, key-term definitions and model answers for the plenary. Follow the sequence to mark each section after students attempt it.

For additional marking guidance, corrections or questions about a resource, email hello@revise360.co.uk. The step-by-step guides cover downloading resources and using an experience.
