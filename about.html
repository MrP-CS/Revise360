Revision students actually want to do

Revise 360 turns each part of the GCSE Computer Science specification into a 360° room. Students look around, read the walls, answer questions at each station, and leave with a score that tells them exactly what to revise. How a lesson runs

  1. Starter on paper. Five minutes, no devices, from the worksheet.
  2. Into the room. Students work the six stations, writing the key fact and challenge answer before they tap each badge.
  3. Final challenge. They predict the answer on the worksheet, then test it in the experience.
  4. Score screen. A breakdown by station: what's secure, what needs revising, what needs reteaching.
  5. Exam practice. Device away, questions answered from memory, marked with the teacher answer sheet.

Questions

How do students get accounts?

You create them. In your dashboard you paste your class list, or import a CSV from your MIS, and a six-digit PIN is generated for each username. Each student gets a login card, printed or shared on Teams, and signs in with just that username and PIN. No email addresses, no real names, and nothing for students to set up.

How do I get a teacher account?

Request a key on the sign-up page from your school email address, and it's sent to you once it's checked. If a colleague at your school already uses Revise 360, ask them to invite you from their dashboard: you'll join their school and see the same classes and results.

Do I need VR headsets?

No. Everything works with a mouse or a touchscreen. Headsets add immersion, not content.

Which headsets work?

Any with a WebXR browser, including Meta Quest 2, 3 and 3S. Open the site in the headset's browser and press Enter VR.

Can I use it for homework?

Yes. Send students the link. Progress saves as they answer and appears on your dashboard.

What if the internet drops mid-lesson?

Work carries on and is saved on the device, then syncs automatically when the connection returns.

Can I get the answer sheets?

Yes: they're for teachers only, so email from your school address and I'll send them.
