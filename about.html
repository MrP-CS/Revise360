One lesson sequence

  1. Starter, then answers. Attempt the question before marking it with the next slide.
  2. Key terms, then definitions. Check terminology before applying it.
  3. Open the experience when instructed. The task slide combines the worksheet and experience instructions for the board.
  4. Explore and write. Complete the stations and challenge, recording the key facts in your own words.
  5. Plenary knowledge checks. Finish the experience in time to answer exam-style questions from memory, then mark and improve using the slides.

Download and teach

Open a topic and use the Lesson PowerPoint and Worksheet buttons on each experience card. Slides are available to teachers and students for classroom use and revision. They include answers, so complete questions before viewing the marking slides.

Questions

Do students need a login?

The current trial uses local usernames and PINs created in the demo teacher dashboard. They work only in the browser where they were created. The homepage experience can be tried without a student login.

Can teachers use Microsoft sign-in?

Teacher Microsoft sign-in is planned but not yet enabled. The current dashboard is a local demo, not a shared school service.

Can I use the resources for homework?

Downloaded worksheets and available lesson slides can support independent revision. Slides include model answers. The current demo does not send results from a student's home device to a teacher's classroom dashboard.

Do I need a VR headset?

No. Use a PC or tablet. Immersive VR requires a compatible WebXR headset and browser; check your devices before teaching.

Are the resources free to republish?

No. Classroom use and personal study are permitted within the terms of use; public redistribution and resale are not.
