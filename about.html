Do we need VR headsets?

No. Use a PC or tablet. Immersive VR is optional and requires a compatible WebXR browser.

Can students use the slides too?

Yes. Available PowerPoints and worksheets support classroom work and independent revision. Attempt questions before revealing the answers.
