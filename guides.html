01 / FOR STUDENTS

Explore with a purpose.

  1. 01

    Do Now: research and discuss

    Begin with the key question on slide 1. Research or discuss it straight away, then attempt the starter and key terms. Check the answers before opening the experience.

  2. 02

    Look, think, write

    Explore the stations. Write the key facts in your own words before answering the interactive questions.

  3. 03

    Apply and check

    Complete the challenge and plenary. Use feedback and model answers to improve your explanations.

  4. 04

    Return to the gaps

    Use Review mode to revisit mistakes. Your first-attempt score stays as a record of your starting point.

02 / FOR TEACHERS

Ready the room.

Download

Choose a topic and download the available PowerPoint and worksheet from the experience card.

Display

Show the Do Now question on slide 1 as students arrive to focus their research or discussion. Mark the starter and key terms, then display the experience instructions.

Discuss

Bring everyone to the plenary. Compare explanations, correct misconceptions and plan the next step.

03 / BEYOND THE LESSON

The same thinking.
A different setting.

At home

Complete the task your teacher has set. Try before checking answers, then return your written work through your usual homework system.

In a small group

Focus on selected stations. Explain your reasoning together, work through misconceptions and finish with a knowledge check.

04 / SCREEN OR HEADSET

Choose your perspective.

Drag or swipe to explore on a screen. In a compatible headset browser, select Enter VR and use your controller to choose a station.

Remove the headset for writing. Follow your teacher’s instructions and the manufacturer’s safety guidance.

NEED A HAND?

Quick fixes.

Where do I find the PowerPoints?

Look for Lesson PowerPoint on the experience card. Decks currently cover Topics 1.1–1.3 and 2.5. Open the downloaded .pptx file in a compatible presentation app.

Where are the answers?

Starter and key-term answers follow their questions. Plenary model answers appear later in the deck.

Why is Enter VR missing?

Your device or browser may not support immersive WebXR. You can still use the experience on a screen.
