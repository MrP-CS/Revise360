Get your teacher key

A teacher key opens your dashboard, where you create your students' logins and print their cards. Everything you create is tied to your school, so you see your classes and nobody else's.

🔑

Your teacher key

A long private string you paste into the dashboard. Keep it to staff: anyone with it can see your school's results.

🪪

Your students' logins

You choose the usernames; PINs are generated. Print a login card for each student, or share them on Teams.

⏱️

Usually same day

Requests are checked by hand from a school email address, then the key and code are emailed to you.

Request a key

We use your email only to send your key and occasional service notices. Nothing is shared with anyone else, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time. See the privacy page.

Request sent. Check your inbox: your teacher key will arrive by email, usually the same working day. If nothing has arrived by the next morning, email hello@revise360.co.uk.

What happens next

  1. Your teacher key arrives by email. Keep it to staff: it opens your school's dashboard.
  2. Create your class logins. In the dashboard, paste your usernames or import a CSV from your MIS. A six-digit PIN is generated for each one.
  3. Hand out the login cards. Print them, or save them to share on Teams or Google Classroom.
  4. Students sign in with just their username and PIN. Nothing else to type, nothing for them to set up.
  5. Watch the results appear on your dashboard, and invite your colleagues so they see the same classes.

Already have a key? Go straight to the dashboard. Lost it? Email from the address you signed up with and it can be reissued. A colleague already using Revise 360? Ask them to invite you from their dashboard instead: you'll join their school automatically and see the same classes.
