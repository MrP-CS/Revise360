Get your teacher key

A teacher key opens your dashboard. It comes with a six-character school code that your students type when they sign in, which is what links their progress to your school and nobody else's.

🔑

Your teacher key

A long private string you paste into the dashboard. Keep it to staff: anyone with it can see your school's results.

🏫

Your school code

Six characters, such as K7M3QP. Safe to put on the board or in a link. Students type it once.

⏱️

Usually same day

Requests are checked by hand from a school email address, then the key and code are emailed to you.

Request a key

We use your email only to send your key and occasional service notices. Nothing is shared with anyone else, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time. See the privacy page.

Request sent. Check your inbox: your teacher key and school code will arrive by email, usually the same working day. If nothing has arrived by the next morning, email hello@revise360.co.uk.

What happens next

  1. You get two things by email: a teacher key and a school code.
  2. Give students the school code, or send them a link with it built in, such as revise360.co.uk/?school=K7M3QP. They type it once when they first sign in.
  3. Students sign in with a username, an optional class, a PIN they choose, and that school code.
  4. Open the dashboard and paste your teacher key. You'll see only students who used your school code.

Already have a key? Go straight to the dashboard. Lost it? Email from the address you signed up with and it can be reissued.
