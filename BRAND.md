# Revise 360 visual identity

The broken ring refers to a 360° view; the cobalt chevron points forward and hints at a code prompt. Use the simple mark in compact spaces and the wordmark when the brand needs to stand alone. Both SVGs have transparent backgrounds.

| Colour | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#101828` | Headings, wordmark, immersive player |
| Canvas | `#F7F7F4` | Learning dashboard background |
| Cobalt | `#2D63FF` | Primary actions, topic numbers, progress |
| Teal | `#08A6A6` | Occasional secondary accent |
| Amber | `#F4B942` | Highlight and feedback |
| Slate | `#667085` | Supporting text |
| Border | `#D9DEE7` | Separators and quiet outlines |

The interface uses IBM Plex Sans with system sans-serif fallbacks. IBM Plex Mono is reserved for topic codes, numbered labels and small metadata. The dashboard is light for reading and scanning; the 360° player remains dark to frame its scenes.

Use [the mark](assets/revise360-mark.svg) for favicons and small controls, and [the complete logo](assets/revise360-logo.svg) for documents or external use. Keep clear space of at least half the mark's width around it. Avoid gradients, emoji icons and decorative cards in the primary learning flow.
