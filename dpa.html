Revise360Processing agreement

Draft data processing agreement

This draft describes proposed arrangements for a future hosted school service. It is not a signed agreement or a statement that the proposed controls are already deployed. Company details, hosting arrangements and the security schedule must be completed and reviewed before use.

Draft for review · Updated 25 September 2026 · Revise 360 Ltd

Before you sign. The current public site is a local demo; see the privacy page for what it does today. The clauses below are proposed terms for a hosted service and require review against the final implementation. Have your own data protection lead or legal adviser review it, and tell us if your trust has a standard form it would rather use: we're happy to work from yours.

Parties

(1) The Customer: the school, academy or trust named in the subscription order, acting as Controller.
(2) Revise 360 Ltd, company number __________, registered in England and Wales at __________ ("Revise 360"), acting as Processor.

1. Definitions and scope

1.1 "Data Protection Law" means the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and any other data protection law applying to the parties, as amended.

1.2 "Personal Data", "Controller", "Processor", "Data Subject", "Processing" and "Personal Data Breach" have the meanings given in Data Protection Law.

1.3 "Services" means access to the Revise 360 website, experiences, lesson PowerPoints, worksheets and teacher dashboard under the Customer's subscription.

1.4 This agreement applies whenever Revise 360 processes Personal Data on the Customer's behalf. Where the Customer hosts its own copy of the backend, Revise 360 processes no pupil Personal Data and only clauses 9, 11 and 13 apply.

1.5 If this agreement conflicts with any other agreement between the parties, this agreement prevails on data protection matters.

2. Roles

2.1 The Customer is the Controller and determines the purposes and means of processing, including which usernames are used and which pupils may use the Services.

2.2 Revise 360 is the Processor and processes Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions.

2.3 The Customer warrants that it has a lawful basis for the processing, has provided the necessary privacy information to pupils and parents, and that the usernames it issues do not contain more Personal Data than necessary.

3. Instructions

3.1 Revise 360 processes Personal Data only: (a) as set out in Annex A; (b) on the Customer's further documented instructions; or (c) where required by law, in which case it will tell the Customer first unless the law forbids it.

3.2 Revise 360 will tell the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Law.

3.3 Revise 360 will not sell Personal Data, use it for advertising or marketing, use it to train machine learning models, or use it for any purpose of its own. Aggregated, anonymised statistics that cannot identify any Data Subject or Customer may be used to improve the Services.

4. Confidentiality and staff

4.1 Revise 360 ensures that anyone authorised to process Personal Data is bound by a duty of confidentiality and has received appropriate data protection guidance.

4.2 Access is limited to those who need it to provide or support the Services.

5. Security

5.1 Revise 360 implements the technical and organisational measures in Annex B, appropriate to the risk, taking account of the state of the art, the costs of implementation and the nature of the processing.

5.2 The final processing schedule must include account identifiers, teacher contact details and authentication data actually used by the hosted service. The prototype stores generated student PINs and teacher keys; this draft does not certify them as hashed or non-readable.

5.3 Revise 360 may update the measures in Annex B provided the level of protection is not reduced.

6. Sub-processors

6.1 The Customer gives general authorisation for the sub-processors listed in Annex C.

6.2 Revise 360 will give the Customer at least 30 days' notice by email before adding or replacing a sub-processor. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period, and if the parties cannot resolve the objection the Customer may terminate the affected Services without penalty and receive a pro-rata refund.

6.3 Revise 360 imposes on each sub-processor obligations equivalent to those in this agreement and remains fully liable for their performance.

7. International transfers

7.1 The storage locations and any international processing must be confirmed in the completed Annex C before the hosted service starts.

7.2 Any transfer outside the UK is made under an adequacy decision, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or another lawful transfer mechanism, and Revise 360 will provide details on request.

8. Assistance to the Customer

8.1 Taking account of the nature of the processing, Revise 360 will assist the Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures in responding to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights.

8.2 Where a request is made directly to Revise 360, it will refer the Data Subject to the Customer and tell the Customer promptly.

8.3 Revise 360 will assist the Customer with data protection impact assessments and any prior consultation with the Information Commissioner, and will provide the supporting material described in its data protection policy.

9. Personal data breaches

9.1 Revise 360 will notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any case within 24 hours, of becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting the Customer's Personal Data.

9.2 The notification will describe the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed, and a contact point.

9.3 Revise 360 will assist the Customer in meeting its own obligations to the Information Commissioner and to Data Subjects, and will not notify any third party without the Customer's consent unless required by law.

10. Deletion and return

10.1 During the subscription, the Customer may delete any record or export its data at any time from the teacher dashboard.

10.2 Progress records untouched for 12 months are deleted automatically.

10.3 On termination, Revise 360 will delete all of the Customer's Personal Data within 30 days, or return it first if the Customer asks within that period, except where storage is required by law. Written confirmation of deletion is provided on request.

11. Audit

11.1 Revise 360 will make available all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28, and will respond to the Customer's reasonable written questions, including completing a supplier security questionnaire, within 30 days.

11.2 The Customer may audit no more than once a year, on 30 days' written notice, during business hours, without unreasonable disruption, and subject to confidentiality. More frequent audits are permitted after a Personal Data Breach or where required by a regulator.

12. Liability

12.1 Each party is liable for its own breaches of Data Protection Law. Liability under this agreement is subject to any limitations in the subscription terms, except that nothing limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited, including for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or fraud.

13. Term, changes and law

13.1 This agreement takes effect when the subscription starts and continues while Revise 360 processes Personal Data for the Customer.

13.2 Material changes will be notified at least 30 days in advance; if the Customer objects it may terminate without penalty.

13.3 This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.

Annex A — details of the processing

ItemDetail
Subject matterProviding revision experiences and progress feedback to pupils and their teachers
DurationThe subscription term, plus up to 30 days for deletion
Nature and purposeCollection, storage, retrieval, display and deletion of quiz progress; making results available to the Customer's teachers
Types of Personal DataTo be finalised: student usernames, class and school identifiers, authentication records, teacher names and contact details, and progress records (scores, answers, personal bests and markers viewed). Categories of Data SubjectPupils of the Customer, and the Customer's teaching staff who hold a dashboard key
Special category dataNone processed
Children's dataYes: pupils typically aged 11 to 16, used under teacher supervision

Annex B — security schedule to be completed

Document and verify the hosted service's authentication, school permissions, encryption, backups, audit records, incident handling, retention and release testing before this agreement is used. The prototype and its local demo are not evidence that these measures are in production.

Annex C — sub-processors to be confirmed

The final schedule must identify the providers used for hosting, accounts, database storage, email and any supporting services, their processing locations and relevant transfer arrangements. Cloudflare is the proposed backend platform; Microsoft would provide teacher authentication if enabled. The current public website is hosted on GitHub Pages.
